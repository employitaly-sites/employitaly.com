# Hiring in Bologna and Emilia-Romagna: Motor Valley and Data Valley

> Bologna and Emilia-Romagna combine high-end engineering with supercomputing. Here is what foreign companies should know before hiring there.

Emilia-Romagna is one of Italy's strongest industrial regions. Bologna sits at its centre. The region is known for two things: Motor Valley and Data Valley. Together they make a good case for foreign companies that need engineers, technicians and data specialists.

## Motor Valley: engineering with a long tradition

The region is home to famous makers of sports cars and motorcycles. Around them sits a dense network of suppliers. These firms make engines, transmissions, bodywork, electronics and tooling. Many are small and highly specialised.

This means the local labour market is deep in mechanical engineering, industrial design, testing and precision manufacturing. People here often change jobs within the same cluster. A foreign company opening a design office or a small production unit can find experienced staff nearby.

The region also has a strong base in packaging machinery and automation. These fields share skills with the motor industry: mechatronics, control systems and quality engineering.

## Data Valley: supercomputing in Bologna

Bologna has become a European centre for high-performance computing. The Tecnopolo district hosts large research computing infrastructure. Universities, research bodies and weather and climate science groups work close together.

For a foreign company this matters in three ways:

- Graduates in physics, mathematics, computer science and engineering come out of the local universities.
- Researchers and engineers with simulation and modelling skills are available.
- Companies in automotive, aerospace, life sciences or energy can build links with research teams.

The link between the two valleys is natural. Car and machine makers use simulation, digital twins and data analysis. The same people can work across both worlds.

## Who should consider hiring here

The region suits companies that need:

- Mechanical, electrical or software engineers close to a manufacturing base
- Simulation and HPC specialists
- Sales and support staff for Italian industrial customers
- A small technical team to serve southern Europe

It is less suited to companies that only need a mailing address. The value is in the skills and the industry around them.

## Local payroll cost to plan for

Italian payroll has national and local parts. Besides national income tax and social contributions, employees pay a municipal surcharge on income tax. In Bologna, the source we use gives a single rate of 0.008. It also gives an exemption threshold of EUR 15000.

This is a small item. But it shows why the work location matters. Each municipality sets its own surcharge. A hire based in Bologna is treated differently from one based in another city. Your payroll must apply the right local rules from the first payslip.

## How an Employer of Record helps

A foreign company without an Italian entity can still hire in Bologna. An Employer of Record (EOR) becomes the legal employer in Italy. You direct the daily work. The EOR handles the contract, payroll, social contributions, local taxes and compliance.

In practice this gives you:

- A compliant Italian employment contract under the right national collective agreement
- Correct payroll, including local surcharges
- Registration with the social security bodies
- Support with work visas and relocation if you bring in talent from abroad
- A way to test the market before you set up a company

This works well for engineering and data roles. You can start with one or two people, see how the team performs, and decide later whether to open a local entity.

## Practical steps

1. Define the role and the work location in the Emilia-Romagna region.
2. Identify the right collective agreement for the sector.
3. Choose the contract type and the start date.
4. Let the EOR issue the contract and register the employee.
5. Run payroll monthly with the correct local taxes.

The work is not complex when it is done in the right order. Mistakes usually come from the wrong contract level or from missing local rules.
