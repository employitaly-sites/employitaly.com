# Meal vouchers in Italy: how to raise net pay within the tax-free limits

> Meal vouchers can raise an Italian employee's net pay without raising payroll cost. Here are the daily tax-free limits and how to use them well.

Italian employees care about take-home pay. When you hire in Italy, a higher gross salary is not always the best way to give them more. Meal vouchers are one of the most common tools to raise net value at a lower cost to you and a better result for the employee.

This article explains the tax-free limits and how to use them well.

## What meal vouchers are

A meal voucher is a benefit the employer gives to cover the cost of lunch on working days. In Italy it is a standard part of many compensation packages. Employees expect it, and candidates often ask about it in interviews.

The benefit has a tax advantage. Up to a daily limit, the value of the voucher is not treated as ordinary salary. Below that limit, the employee receives the full value without income tax on it. The same value paid as cash salary would be taxed.

## The tax-free limits

The limit depends on the format of the voucher.

- Electronic meal vouchers: up to EUR 10 per day.
- Paper meal vouchers: up to EUR 4 per day.

These figures come from a secondary source that summarises the latest budget law changes. Check them with your Italian payroll provider before you set up the plan, because the rules can change.

The difference between the two formats is large. The electronic limit is much higher than the paper one. For a foreign company, the practical lesson is simple: choose electronic vouchers.

## Why the format matters

If you give a voucher worth more than the daily limit, the part above the limit is treated as taxable income. It is then taxed like salary, and it may also attract social contributions. You lose the efficiency you were looking for.

So the goal is to stay at or below the limit. A voucher worth exactly the limit gives the employee the most tax-free value per day. A voucher worth more than the limit gives little extra value for the cost.

With paper vouchers, the low limit means the tax-free benefit is small. With electronic vouchers, the same benefit can be much larger. For the employee, the net gain over a year is clear.

## How to use vouchers to raise net pay

Here is a simple way to think about it.

1. Decide the total package you want to offer.
2. Set the voucher at the electronic daily limit.
3. Keep the rest as base salary.

The employee gets a package that looks better in take-home terms. The voucher part is tax-free, so each euro in vouchers is worth a full euro to the employee. Each euro in salary is worth less after tax.

This is useful when you compete for talent. Candidates compare offers on what reaches their bank account or their wallet. A package with vouchers at the limit often beats a higher gross salary with no benefits.

## Points to check

Vouchers are paid per day actually worked, not per calendar day. They do not apply on days of leave or when the employee does not work. Your payroll must track this.

The limit is per day. It is not a monthly allowance you can spend in one go. Plan the benefit around expected working days.

Remote work needs a decision. Employees who work from home may still receive vouchers, depending on the contract, the collective agreement and company policy. Set the rule in writing from the start so there is no dispute later.

Your collective agreement may already define a meal benefit. Read it before you design your own. The agreement may require a minimum or set a specific format.

## What this means for a foreign employer

You do not need a local entity to offer this benefit if you hire through an Employer of Record. The EOR is the legal employer in Italy. It handles the voucher provider, the payroll treatment and the reporting. You set the budget and the policy. The EOR applies the limits correctly.

This removes a common risk. A wrong voucher value or format can create tax exposure for the employer. With a local payroll team that knows the current limits, you avoid it.

## Summary

Meal vouchers are a cheap way to raise net pay in Italy. Use the electronic format, because its tax-free limit is much higher than the paper one. Keep the value at or below the daily limit. Pay it only for days worked. Confirm the current figures with your payroll provider before you launch.
