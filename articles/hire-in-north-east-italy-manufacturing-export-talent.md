# Hiring in Venice, Padua and Italy's North-East industrial belt

> How foreign companies can hire manufacturing and export talent in Venice, Padua and the North-East, and why an Employer of Record fits.

The North-East of Italy is one of the country's main industrial regions. Venice, Padua and the towns around them are home to a dense network of manufacturers. Many are small and mid-sized firms. Many sell most of what they make abroad.

If your company wants to build or expand a team here, the people you need already work in this system. This article explains what kind of talent you can find, and how to hire it without opening a local company.

## Why the North-East matters for manufacturing

The area is built around production. You find machinery, metalworking, furniture, fashion and footwear, food processing, packaging, and components for larger supply chains. Companies often cluster by sector. Suppliers, toolmakers and service firms sit close to each other. Knowledge moves between them.

This has a practical effect on hiring. A production engineer in Padua may have worked for several local firms. A technician near Venice may know how a whole supply chain runs, not just one plant. That depth is hard to find in a new market.

## Export skills are part of the culture

Many firms in the region have sold abroad for generations. Their staff are used to working with foreign clients. They know export paperwork, shipping terms, quality standards and customer audits. Many speak English. Some speak German, French or other languages that matter for their sectors.

If you want to sell into Europe, or source from it, these are useful people to have on your side. Typical profiles include:

- Export sales managers and area sales managers
- Customer service and order management staff
- Supply chain and logistics planners
- Quality managers and compliance specialists
- Production and process engineers
- Maintenance and automation technicians
- Procurement and supplier development staff

## What foreign companies usually want to do

There are a few common cases.

The first is a sales presence. You want someone near your customers or distributors in Italy and nearby markets. A local export sales person can open doors faster than a remote team.

The second is sourcing and quality. You buy from Italian suppliers and need someone on the ground. That person visits plants, checks samples, follows orders and solves problems early.

The third is engineering and technical support. You need people who understand Italian machinery or production methods, and who can support clients in the field.

The fourth is a small regional office. You do not need a large team. You need two or three people who can start quickly.

## The hiring problem

Hiring in Italy is not like hiring in many other countries. Employment contracts follow national collective agreements. These set pay levels, job grades, notice periods, leave and other terms. The right agreement depends on the sector and the role. Payroll, social contributions and reporting also follow local rules.

If you set up a local entity just to hire one or two people, you take on a lot of work. You need to register, find an accountant, set up payroll and handle compliance. That can take time and cost more than the hire is worth, especially if you are still testing the market.

## How an Employer of Record helps

An Employer of Record (EOR) hires the person on your behalf in Italy. The worker is a legal employee in Italy, with an Italian contract that follows the correct collective agreement. You direct their daily work. The EOR handles the employment side.

In practice, that means:

- A compliant Italian employment contract
- Payroll and social contributions
- Onboarding and employee paperwork
- Ongoing administration and support
- A clear way to end the arrangement if the plan changes

You do not need a local company. You can hire the person you found and start working with them.

This fits the North-East well. Many roles here are technical or tied to a specific sector. You often want to move fast to secure someone with the right profile. An EOR lets you do that without waiting for a local entity to be ready.

## Relocation and international staff

Sometimes the right person is not already in the North-East. You may want to bring in someone from your home country or from elsewhere. In that case, visas, work permits and relocation come into play. An EOR can support this too, so the employee arrives with the right status and the right paperwork in place.

## Tips for a good start

- Define the role clearly. Say whether it is sales, technical, quality or operations.
- Check which collective agreement applies before you set a salary.
- Decide how the person will work. On site, at a customer, or from home.
- Plan visits to local suppliers or customers early. Face-to-face contact matters in this region.
- Start small. One good hire can show you whether the market works for you.

## Summary

Venice, Padua and the wider North-East offer manufacturing know-how and export experience in one place. You can tap into it without building a local company first. An Employer of Record gives you a fast and compliant way to hire.
