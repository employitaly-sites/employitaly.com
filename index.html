What’s included in the €250

One monthly fee per employee covers the whole employment relationship in Italy. The salary, the employer’s social contributions and any benefits you choose are passed through at cost, with no mark-up.

Italian employment contract

Drafted under the right national collective agreement (CCNL) for the role, with probation, notice, working hours and leave set out clearly.

Monthly payroll

Payslips, income tax withholding, INPS and INAIL contributions, 13th month (and 14th where the CCNL provides it), severance (TFR) accrual and year-end tax forms.

HR administration

Onboarding and registrations, leave and sick-leave records, expense reimbursements, contract changes and offboarding.

Compliance

CCNL renewals, law changes and filing deadlines tracked for you. We are the legal employer and carry the Italian employer obligations.

One invoice, your currency

A single monthly invoice in your local currency, with the EUR reference price and the exchange rate source shown on it.

A real team

An Italian team that answers in English and in the language of our country sites, with data kept on servers in the European Union.

How it works, in three steps

  1. Tell us who you want to hireRole, city, start date and planned salary. Within 24 hours (one business day) you get the full monthly and annual cost, line by line.
  2. We sign the Italian contractWe pick the CCNL, draft the contract and register the employee. Non-EU hires start the work permit or EU Blue Card process in parallel.
  3. Monthly payroll, one invoiceYou approve hours and expenses; we pay salary, taxes and contributions on time and send you the payroll reports.

Common questions

Do we need an Italian company?

No. We are the employer in Italy; you direct the work as you would with any team member.

Which countries do you work with?

Any country. Many clients find a site in their own language on our brands page.

How fast can someone start?

EU citizens can usually start within days of a signed offer. Visa cases take longer, depending on the permit.

Can we convert a contractor to an employee?

Yes. We move them to an Italian employment contract, which removes the risk of a disguised employment relationship.
